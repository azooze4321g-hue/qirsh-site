Last updated / آخر تحديث: 13 October 2026  ·  App version 1.0

The short version. Qirsh is a budgeting app, and the budgeting works entirely on your phone. You do not need an account to use it, and your financial data — your income, your transactions, your goals — is stored on your device, encrypted.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. We never read your text messages.

Without an account, the only thing that leaves your phone is anonymous crash and update information. If you sign in, three more things can, each only when you choose it: what you ask the assistant (your question and the budget figures needed to answer it), your subscription status, and — if you subscribe to Qirsh Pro and turn it on — a backup of your data so you can restore it on a new phone. All of it is described in full below.

1. Who we are

Qirsh (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is operated by Qirsh Labs, Saudi Arabia. For anything in this policy, contact us at support@qirshapp.com.

For the purposes of Saudi Arabia’s Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Qirsh Labs is the data controller for the limited personal data described here.

2. What Qirsh stores on your phone

Everything you enter into Qirsh is written to your device’s own storage:

  • Your income, pay-cycle setup and payday
  • Transactions you add, edit or import (amount, date, category, and any note you write)
  • Categories, recurring bills, savings entries and wishes
  • Your settings — language, theme, currency, whether decimals are shown

This data stays on your device unless you turn on cloud backup (§4). It is stored in an encrypted database: Qirsh generates a random AES-256 key on first launch, stores that key in your operating system’s secure keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), and never ships it inside the app. On Android the app also sets allowBackup=false, which excludes this data from the operating system’s automatic cloud backups.

If you never create an account, Qirsh works fully and we receive none of this data — ever.

3. Bank SMS: read entirely on your device

Qirsh can turn a bank SMS into a transaction. It is important to be precise about how:

  • You paste the message yourself. The app does not request permission to read your messages, and it cannot read them.
  • The text is parsed on your device. The amount, date and merchant are extracted by code running on your phone. The message is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Nothing is saved automatically. Every parsed row is shown to you for review, and you choose what to keep.

The pasted text is not stored after you leave the screen. Only the transaction you approve is saved, and only on your device.

4. Cloud backup (Qirsh Pro only, and off by default)

If you subscribe to Qirsh Pro and sign in, you can enable cloud backup so your data survives a lost or replaced phone. This is optional. Backup happens only if you turn it on, and signing in is never required to use Qirsh.

What is uploaded

Two files: one containing your settings and plan, one containing your transactions. They are sent as structured data, together with an identifier for the device that sent them (used only to warn you when two devices have made conflicting changes) and timestamps.

Where it is stored

On Supabase, our hosting provider, in their Frankfurt, Germany (eu-central-1) region. Each user’s row is protected by database access rules so that a signed-in user can read and write only their own row.

How it is protected — stated precisely

WhereProtection
On your phoneEncrypted with AES-256; the key lives in the OS secure keystore, not in the app.
In transitEncrypted with TLS (HTTPS) between your phone and our provider.
In the cloudStored in a row only your account can access, on infrastructure encrypted at rest by the provider.

An honest limitation: the cloud copy is not end-to-end encrypted. It is protected by access controls and by our provider’s encryption, but it is not mathematically impossible for us or our hosting provider to read it. We do not do so, and we have no feature, tool or business reason to — but we would rather tell you that plainly than imply a guarantee we do not provide. Your on-device copy is the encrypted one.

How long we keep it

Your backup is kept until you overwrite it (the next backup replaces the previous one) or until you delete your account, at which point it is deleted along with everything else (§10).

5. Account data

An account is needed only for the assistant, for buying Qirsh Pro, and for cloud backup. When you create one we hold:

  • Your email address, or the identifier your provider gives us if you use Sign in with Apple or Google. If you use Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we only ever see the relay address.
  • A display name, if you set one.
  • Your subscription status and its expiry, so the app knows whether Pro is active.
  • Account creation and update timestamps.

We do not hold your payment details. Subscriptions are purchased and billed by Apple or Google through their app stores; they process the payment and we only receive whether your subscription is active.

To keep track of subscriptions we use RevenueCat. It receives your account’s ID — a random identifier, not your email — and the record of your Qirsh purchases from Apple or Google, and it tells our server whether your subscription is active.

6. The assistant

Qirsh includes an assistant you can ask about your budget. It is optional and needs an account. Nothing is sent to it until you send it a message. It runs on an AI model provided by OpenAI.

What is sent when you send a message

  • Your message, and the earlier messages of the same chat, so it can follow the conversation.
  • A short summary of your budget that the app prepares: what you planned, spent and have left since your last salary, your savings and goal, your bills, your recorded balance, your category totals, and your first name if you entered one.
  • When your question needs more detail, the figures that answer it — for example what you spent in one category or at one shop, the bills coming up, or your wishes — together with the names and descriptions you typed for shops, bills, categories and wishes.
  • Any notes you chose to save for the assistant, and your language and tone settings.

It is not sent your email address, the bank messages you paste for import, or any payment details.

Where it goes

Your phone sends this to our server, which checks your account and your usage allowance and passes it to OpenAI in the United States. The reply comes back the same way. Each message is also checked by OpenAI’s safety filter.

What is kept, and where

WhereWhat is kept
On your phoneYour chats and saved notes, encrypted with the rest of your data. You can delete a chat or a note in the app.
On our serverNo message text and no replies. Only counters: how much of your usage allowance you have used, and when.

Saved notes are part of your settings, so if you turn on cloud backup (§4) they are included in it. Chats are not.

What to keep in mind

The assistant’s numbers come from your own records in the app, but its wording is written by an AI model and can be wrong. It is not financial advice (see our Terms). Please do not type into the chat anything you would not want processed outside your phone, such as card numbers or passwords.

7. Crash reports

Qirsh sends anonymous crash and error reports to Sentry, so we can find and fix bugs. This is deliberately built to carry no personal or financial information:

  • No user identity is attached — the user field is removed from every report before it is sent.
  • Anything that looks financial is redacted before sending: fields whose names mention income, balance, amount, savings, transaction, note, salary, merchant, IBAN or account are replaced with [redacted], in the error itself and in the diagnostic trail.
  • Long text values and request URLs are redacted rather than transmitted.
  • We do not enable Sentry’s optional personal-data collection (which would otherwise attach your IP address or email).
  • Sentry also receives an anonymous count of app sessions (that the app was opened, which version, and whether it crashed), so we can see what share of sessions end in a crash.

What remains is essentially: which version of the app crashed, on what kind of device, and where in the code. Sentry processes this in the European Union.

8. App updates

The app checks Expo’s update service (u.expo.dev) for improvements it can install without a full re-download from the store. This request tells the service which app version and device platform is asking, with a random identifier for this installation of the app; it carries none of your financial data and no account information.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. Ever, to anyone.
  • We do not show advertising, and the app contains no advertising SDK.
  • We do not use advertising or behaviour-tracking tools, and we do not build a profile of your behaviour. There is no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK or similar in the app.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not access your messages, contacts, camera, photos, microphone or location, and Qirsh never asks for them.
  • We do not send anything to an artificial intelligence service unless you use the assistant; what it receives when you do is described in §6. The bank messages you paste are never sent to it.

10. Your rights

Under the Saudi PDPL and, where it applies to you, the GDPR, you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, obtain a copy of it, object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to a regulator — in Saudi Arabia, the Saudi Data & Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA).

In practice, most of these you can exercise yourself, immediately, without asking us:

  • Access and correction — your data is on your phone, in the app, and every entry can be edited or deleted there.
  • Deletion — deleting the app removes the local data. To delete your account and everything we hold in the cloud, use Profile → Account → Delete account & data in the app. That permanently deletes your account record, your subscription record, your assistant usage counters and your cloud backup. It cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Apple or Google: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account. The purchase records themselves stay with the store, and with RevenueCat under your random account ID.

For anything you cannot do in the app, write to support@qirshapp.com. We will respond within 30 days.

12. International transfers

Qirsh is built for people in Saudi Arabia, and our providers have no Saudi region. Account records, cloud backups and assistant usage counters are therefore stored in Frankfurt, Germany (Supabase), and crash reports are processed in the European Union (Sentry). What you send to the assistant is processed in the United States (OpenAI), and so are subscription records (RevenueCat) and update checks (Expo).

This means your personal data is transferred outside the Kingdom. We rely on the transfer conditions permitted by the PDPL and its implementing regulations, supported by contractual safeguards with our providers, and we keep the transferred data to the minimum described in this policy. If a Saudi region becomes available, we intend to migrate.

يتحقق التطبيق من خدمة التحديثات u.expo.dev بحثاً عن تحسينات يقدر ينزّلها بدون إعادة تحميل كاملة من المتجر. هذا الطلب يخبر الخدمة بإصدار التطبيق ونوع المنصة، مع معرّف عشوائي لهذا التثبيت من التطبيق؛ وما يحمل أي بيانات مالية ولا معلومات حساب.

٩. وش ما نسوّيه

  • ما نبيع ولا نؤجّر بياناتك الشخصية. أبداً، ولا لأي جهة.
  • ما نعرض إعلانات، وما في التطبيق أي حزمة إعلانات.
  • ما نستخدم أدوات إعلانات أو تتبّع سلوك، وما نبني ملفاً عن سلوكك. ما في Google Analytics ولا Firebase Analytics ولا حزمة Facebook ولا ما شابه.
  • ما نستخدم معرّفات إعلانية، وما نتتبّعك عبر تطبيقات أو مواقع ثانية.
  • ما نوصل إلى رسائلك أو جهات اتصالك أو الكاميرا أو الصور أو الميكروفون أو موقعك، وقرش ما يطلبها أبداً.
  • ما نرسل أي شيء لخدمة ذكاء اصطناعي إلا إذا استخدمت المساعد، واللي يوصله وقتها مشروح في فقرة ٦. ورسائل البنك اللي تلصقها ما تُرسل له أبداً.

١٠. حقوقك

بموجب نظام حماية البيانات الشخصية السعودي، و— حيثما ينطبق عليك — النظام الأوروبي، لك الحق في الوصول إلى بياناتك وتصحيحها وحذفها والحصول على نسخة منها والاعتراض على معالجتها أو تقييدها، وتقديم شكوى إلى الجهة الرقابية — وفي السعودية هي الهيئة السعودية للبيانات والذكاء الاصطناعي (سدايا).

وعملياً، أغلب هذي الحقوق تقدر تمارسها بنفسك وفوراً وبدون ما تطلب منّا شيء:

  • الوصول والتصحيح — بياناتك على جوالك داخل التطبيق، وكل إدخال يمكن تعديله أو حذفه من هناك.
  • الحذف — حذف التطبيق يزيل البيانات المحلية. ولحذف حسابك وكل ما نحتفظ به في السحابة، استخدم الملف ← الحساب ← حذف الحساب والبيانات داخل التطبيق. هذا يحذف نهائياً سجل حسابك وسجل اشتراكك وعدّادات استخدامك للمساعد ونسختك الاحتياطية. ولا يمكن التراجع عنه. وحذف الحساب ما يلغي اشتراكاً اشتريته عبر Apple أو Google: ألغِه من حسابك في App Store أو Google Play. وسجلات الشراء نفسها تبقى عند المتجر، وعند RevenueCat تحت معرّف حسابك العشوائي.

ولأي شيء ما تقدر تسوّيه داخل التطبيق، راسلنا على support@qirshapp.com، وبنرد خلال ٣٠ يوم.
