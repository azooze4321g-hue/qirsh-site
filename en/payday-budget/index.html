A budget that follows your payday

A calendar month does not always match the time your salary needs to last. Qirsh helps you plan around your pay schedule, with bills and savings considered together.

Start with your actual pay schedule

If payday is the 25th, planning only from the first to the last day of the month can split one salary across two views. In Qirsh, set your pay schedule and review the period dates so your plan follows the time you are budgeting for.

Make room for commitments

Add the bills and instalments you expect to pay. If you are planning towards a savings goal, include that too. A bank balance alone does not show how much is available for everyday spending after those commitments.

Keep the plan connected to real life

Log spending and keep your recorded balance, income and bills up to date. Expected salary is not the same as money already received. When your circumstances change, review the plan rather than assuming the old daily amount still fits.

Example: payday on the 25th

You are planning through to the next payday, with rent due on the 1st. That rent belongs in your planning even though the calendar month changes before your next salary. The exact spending room depends on your recorded money, commitments and selected plan.
