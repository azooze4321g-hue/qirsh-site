See what a purchase would change before you buy

Having enough money to pay is only part of the decision. Qirsh lets you preview a purchase against your current plan so you can see its effect before committing.

Enter the amount you are considering

Use the purchase check to explore a possible expense. The preview is hypothetical: checking an amount does not record an expense or move money.

Read the effect on the rest of your plan

Look at the remaining budget and the room left for the days ahead, not just whether the purchase fits today. Bills, your selected plan and the records you have entered affect the result.

Decide, then keep your records current

You can buy, wait or consider a smaller amount. If you do buy, record the actual expense. The preview helps you compare consequences; it cannot guarantee that an unrecorded bill or later expense will fit.

Compare two possible purchases

Suppose you are considering headphones for 300 riyals or a different pair for 180. Preview each amount and compare the effect on your remaining budget. These are example prices, not a claim that either purchase fits your personal plan.
