Know what is left in your budget today

Your account balance answers one question. Your daily budget answers another: how much room does your current plan leave for today? Qirsh brings that daily view together with your recorded expenses.

A daily budget is not your bank balance

Some of your recorded money may already be needed for bills or a savings plan. Qirsh helps you see daily spending room in that wider context. It does not connect to your bank to verify that every expense or balance is current.

Record the small purchases too

Coffee, groceries and transport all add up. Logging purchases keeps your daily view useful, while category and shop breakdowns help you understand where the money went. You can also bring existing records from Excel or CSV.

Review the days ahead

Spending today can change the room left for the rest of the period. Use the daily view alongside your remaining-period budget, and review your plan when income, bills or savings targets change. A displayed amount is planning guidance based on the records you provide.

A useful daily check-in

Before heading out, review today’s budget. After buying lunch or groceries, record the expense and check what remains. If a larger purchase is coming up, preview it before deciding rather than treating your account balance as the whole budget.
