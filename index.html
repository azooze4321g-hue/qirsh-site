Know what you can spend today.

Qirsh is a budget app built around your salary. It sets aside your bills and savings first, then spreads what is left over the days until your next payday. One clear number for today.

  • Works without an account
  • Arabic and English
  • No ads

How Qirsh thinks

Three ideas do most of the work.

1

Your payday, not the calendar

If your salary lands on the 25th, your month starts on the 25th. Paid monthly or every two weeks, Qirsh follows your pay.

2

One number a day

Spend less today and tomorrow's number grows. Spend more and it shrinks. Tap the question mark to see exactly how it was worked out.

3

Paste the bank message

Paste a purchase SMS and Qirsh reads the amount, the shop and the date on your phone. It never asks for permission to read your messages.

Everything in one place

The parts of your money that usually live in five different apps and one notes file.

  • Bills and instalmentsRent, subscriptions and monthly instalments are set aside before your daily number is worked out.
  • Savings and wishesSet a savings goal, or save toward something you want, and see how far along you are.
  • Where the money wentThis month's spending by category and by shop.
  • Can I afford it?Type a price and Qirsh tells you whether it fits, using the same numbers as your budget.
  • Ask QirshAn assistant that answers from your own numbers, in Saudi Arabic or English.

Your money is your business

A budget app sees everything you spend, so here is what Qirsh does with it.

  • No account needed. The budget works fully offline.
  • Your data is encrypted on your phone.
  • No ads, and we do not sell your data.
  • Cloud backup is optional and stays off until you turn it on.
  • Bank messages are read on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

كيف يفكّر قرش

ثلاث أفكار تسوّي أغلب الشغل.

1

راتبك، مو التقويم

إذا راتبك ينزل يوم 25، شهرك يبدأ يوم 25. شهري أو كل أسبوعين، قرش يمشي مع راتبك.

2

رقم واحد كل يوم

صرفت أقل اليوم؟ رقم بكرة يكبر. صرفت أكثر؟ يصغر. واضغط علامة الاستفهام وتشوف كيف انحسب بالضبط.

3

الصق رسالة البنك

فلوسك شأنك

تطبيق الميزانية يشوف كل صرفك، فهذا اللي يسوّيه قرش فيه.

  • ما يحتاج حساب. الميزانية تشتغل كاملة بدون إنترنت.
  • بياناتك مشفّرة داخل جوالك.
  • بدون إعلانات، وما نبيع بياناتك.
  • النسخ الاحتياطي السحابي اختياري، وما يشتغل إلا إذا شغّلته.
  • رسائل البنك تُقرأ داخل جوالك وما تنرفع لأي مكان.
  • هذا الموقع بدون كوكيز وبدون أدوات تتبّع.
